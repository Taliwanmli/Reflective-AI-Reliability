# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a respectful, careful, and academically honest environment. Participation is welcome regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint when it is expressed constructively.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss methods and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and limitations plainly.
- Respect privacy and never solicit or publish personal reflections for this project.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Accept correction when a claim or artefact is unsupported.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate privacy violations, fabricated evidence, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, without promising outcomes that cannot be guaranteed.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
